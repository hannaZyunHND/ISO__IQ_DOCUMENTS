# SOP-06.4: GIÁO DỤC MÔI TRƯỜNG

## 1. MỤC ĐÍCH
Quy định quy trình giáo dục môi trường cho học sinh và cộng đồng, đảm bảo:
- Nâng cao nhận thức môi trường
- Hình thành thói quen xanh
- Tích hợp vào chương trình học
- Hành động cụ thể
- Lan tỏa cộng đồng

## 3. ECO-CLUBS & EXTRACURRICULARS

### 3.1. Green Club

#### 3.1.1. Structure
```
Membership:
- Open to all students
- 50-100 members
- Meetings: Weekly (1 giờ)

Leadership:
- President
- Vice President
- Project leads (waste, energy, water, campaigns)

Faculty advisor: 1-2 teachers
```

#### 3.1.2. Activities
```
Regular:
- Waste monitoring (bin checks)
- Energy patrols (lights off?)
- Water checks (leaks?)
- Awareness campaigns
- Recycling drives

Projects:
- School garden
- Composting program
- Tree planting
- Beach cleanups
- Community outreach

Events:
- Earth Day celebration
- World Environment Day
- Sustainability week
- Eco-fairs
```

### 3.2. Special programs

#### 3.2.1. Eco-leaders
```
Program:
- Select 20-30 students
- Training program (6 sessions)
- Certification
- Lead initiatives
- Mentor others

Selection:
- Application + interview
- Passion for environment
- Leadership potential
- Commitment
```

## 4. CAMPAIGNS & EVENTS

### 4.1. Annual campaigns

#### 4.1.1. Earth Week (Tháng 4)
```
Theme: "Protect Our Planet"

Activities:
- Assembly (keynote speaker)
- Competitions (poster, video, essay)
- Workshops (zero waste, upcycling)
- Tree planting
- Beach/park cleanup
- Eco-fair (student projects showcase)
- Pledges (personal commitments)

Impact: Whole school engaged
```

#### 4.1.2. Plastic-Free July
```
Challenge: Eliminate single-use plastic

Activities:
- Awareness campaign
- Plastic-free lunch challenge
- Reusable alternatives
- Track progress
- Rewards (classes)

Goal: Sustain habits beyond July
```

#### 4.1.3. Other campaigns
```
- Walk/Bike to School Week (Giảm carbon)
- Lights Out Day (Energy saving)
- Water Conservation Week
- E-waste Collection Drive
- Meatless Monday (Climate impact)
```

### 4.2. Community events

#### 4.2.1. Eco-fair
```
Audience: Students, parents, community

Exhibits:
- Student projects
- Sustainability initiatives
- Green vendors
- Workshops
- Activities (kids)

Goals:
- Showcase work
- Educate community
- Recruit support
- Partnerships
```

- Waste reduction: ≥ 5%/năm

- Student-led initiatives: ≥ 5/năm

- Eco-school certification: Achieved
- Awards: ≥ 2/năm
