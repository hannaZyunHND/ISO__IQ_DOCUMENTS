## 4. CARBON FOOTPRINT CALCULATION

### 4.1. Scope definition

#### 4.1.1. GHG Protocol scopes
```
Scope 1 - Direct emissions:
- School vehicles (nếu có)
- Generators
- Gas (heating, cooking)

Scope 2 - Indirect emissions:
- Purchased electricity

Scope 3 - Other indirect:
- Commuting (students, staff)
- Purchased goods
- Waste disposal
- Business travel
```

### 4.2. Calculation

#### 4.2.1. Formula
```
Carbon footprint = Σ(Activity data × Emission factor)

Example - Electricity:
- Consumption: 500,000 kWh/năm
- Emission factor: 0.85 kg CO2e/kWh (VN grid)
- Emissions: 500,000 × 0.85 = 425 tấn CO2e

Calculate for all sources, sum total
```

#### 4.2.2. Tools
```
Use:
- Carbon calculators (GHG Protocol)
- Spreadsheet templates
- Software (sustainability platforms)

Emission factors:
- IPCC database
- National factors (Ministry of Environment)
- Industry standards
```

#### 5.2.2. Certifications & ratings

**Eco-Schools**
```
Program: International green school certification

Requirements:
- Eco-Committee formed
- Environmental review conducted
- Action plan developed
- Monitor và evaluate
- Curriculum integration
- Community involvement
- Eco-code created

Levels: Bronze, Silver, Green Flag
Apply: Annually
```

**LEED for Schools**
```
Certification: Building sustainability

Categories:
- Location và transportation
- Sustainable sites
- Water efficiency
- Energy và atmosphere
- Materials và resources
- Indoor environmental quality
- Innovation

Levels: Certified, Silver, Gold, Platinum
Apply: For new/renovated buildings
```

**ISO 14001**
```
Standard: Environmental Management System

Requirements:
- Environmental policy
- Planning (objectives, targets)
- Implementation (resources, training)
- Monitoring và measurement
- Management review
- Continuous improvement

Certification: Third-party audit
Recertification: Every 3 năm
```
