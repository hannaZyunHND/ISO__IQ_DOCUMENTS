# SOP-04.2: ĐÀM PHÁN VÀ KÝ KẾT HỢP TÁC

## 1. MỤC ĐÍCH
Quy định quy trình đàm phán và ký kết thỏa thuận hợp tác chiến lược, đảm bảo:
- Thỏa thuận công bằng và win-win
- Điều khoản rõ ràng và khả thi
- Rủi ro được quản lý
- Tuân thủ pháp luật
- Nền tảng vững chắc cho hợp tác

## 2. QUY TRÌNH ĐÀM PHÁN

### 2.1. Bước 1: Chuẩn bị (2-3 tuần)

#### 2.1.1. Define objectives
```
BATNA (Best Alternative To Negotiated Agreement):
- Phương án tốt nhất nếu không deal
- Điểm dừng đàm phán

Target outcome:
- Kết quả lý tưởng
- Must-haves vs. nice-to-haves

Walk-away point:
- Điều kiện tối thiểu
- Deal-breakers
```

#### 2.1.2. Develop proposal
```
Partnership Proposal bao gồm:

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (1 trang)
2. ABOUT US (2 trang)
3. PARTNERSHIP VISION (1 trang)
4. PROPOSED COLLABORATION (3-5 trang):
   - Scope và activities
   - Timeline
   - Roles và responsibilities
   - Expected outcomes
5. VALUE PROPOSITION (2 trang):
   - For partner
   - For us
   - For students/community
6. GOVERNANCE (1 trang)
7. FINANCIAL TERMS (1 trang)
8. NEXT STEPS (1 trang)
```

#### 2.1.3. Build negotiation team
```
Team:
- Lead negotiator (Hiệu trưởng hoặc Phó HT)
- Subject matter expert (Trưởng phòng liên quan)
- Legal counsel
- Financial advisor (nếu cần)

Roles:
- Lead: Strategy và final decisions
- SME: Technical details
- Legal: Contract terms
- Financial: Numbers
```

### 2.2. Bước 2: Negotiation meetings (2-4 tuần)

#### 2.2.1. Meeting structure
```
Meeting 1: Exploratory (90 phút)
├─ Share proposals
├─ Discuss vision và goals
├─ Identify common ground
└─ Outline key issues

Meeting 2-3: Substantive (2-3 giờ each)
├─ Negotiate scope
├─ Discuss terms
├─ Address concerns
└─ Find compromises

Meeting 4: Closing (90 phút)
├─ Finalize terms
├─ Review draft agreement
├─ Confirm commitments
└─ Sign MOU/LOI
```

#### 2.2.2. Negotiation tactics
```
Preparation:
- Know your BATNA
- Understand their interests
- Prepare concessions
- Practice scenarios

During:
- Listen actively
- Ask open questions
- Focus on interests, not positions
- Create value before claiming
- Build rapport

Closing:
- Summarize agreements
- Clarify ambiguities
- Document everything
- Set next steps
```

### 2.3. Bước 3: Due diligence (2-3 tuần)

#### 2.3.1. Legal due diligence
```
Verify:
□ Legal status và registration
□ Licenses và permits
□ Litigation history
□ Compliance record
□ Ownership structure
□ Authority to contract
```

#### 2.3.2. Financial due diligence
```
Review:
□ Financial statements (3 years)
□ Audit reports
□ Credit rating
□ Cash flow
□ Liabilities
□ Insurance coverage
```

#### 2.3.3. Operational due diligence
```
Assess:
□ Track record với partners khác
□ Delivery capability
□ Quality standards
□ Systems và processes
□ Key personnel
□ Risk factors
```

#### 2.3.4. Reputational due diligence
```
Check:
□ Media coverage
□ Social media sentiment
□ Customer reviews
□ Industry reputation
□ Awards và recognition
□ Controversies
```

### 2.4. Bước 4: Contract drafting (2-3 tuần)

#### 2.4.1. Agreement structure
```
PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT

1. PARTIES
2. RECITALS (Background)
3. DEFINITIONS
4. PURPOSE AND OBJECTIVES
5. SCOPE OF COLLABORATION
6. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
7. GOVERNANCE
8. FINANCIAL TERMS
9. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
10. CONFIDENTIALITY
11. TERM AND TERMINATION
12. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
13. GENERAL PROVISIONS
14. SIGNATURES

SCHEDULES:
- Schedule A: Detailed scope
- Schedule B: Budget
- Schedule C: Timeline
- Schedule D: KPIs
```

#### 2.4.2. Key clauses
```
Must-have clauses:
□ Clear scope và deliverables
□ Performance metrics
□ Payment terms
□ IP ownership
□ Confidentiality
□ Termination conditions
□ Liability và indemnity
□ Dispute resolution
□ Governing law
```

#### 2.4.3. Review process
```
Draft 1: Legal counsel drafts
    ↓
Review: Internal stakeholders
    ↓
Draft 2: Incorporate feedback
    ↓
Send: To partner for review
    ↓
Negotiate: Terms và wording
    ↓
Final: Agreed version
```

### 2.5. Bước 5: Execution (1 tuần)

#### 2.5.1. Signing ceremony
```
Preparation:
- Finalize documents (2 copies)
- Schedule ceremony
- Invite stakeholders
- Prepare PR materials

Event:
- Formal signing
- Photo opportunity
- Press release
- Celebration
```

#### 2.5.2. Post-signing
```
Immediate:
□ Distribute signed copies
□ Announce internally
□ Announce externally
□ Setup governance structure
□ Kickoff planning

Within 1 month:
□ Kickoff meeting
□ Detailed planning
□ Resource allocation
□ Communication plan
```

## 3. NEGOTIATION BEST PRACTICES

### 3.1. Preparation
```
✅ Research thoroughly
✅ Know your BATNA
✅ Understand their interests
✅ Prepare multiple scenarios
✅ Practice với team
```

### 3.2. During negotiation
```
✅ Listen more than talk
✅ Ask "why" to understand interests
✅ Focus on creating value
✅ Be flexible on how, firm on what
✅ Build relationship
✅ Document everything

❌ Rush to agreement
❌ Make ultimatums
❌ Negotiate against yourself
❌ Ignore red flags
❌ Agree under pressure
```

### 3.3. Common pitfalls
```
❌ Inadequate preparation
❌ Emotional decision-making
❌ Ignoring cultural differences
❌ Vague terms
❌ Unrealistic expectations
❌ Poor communication
❌ Skipping due diligence
```
