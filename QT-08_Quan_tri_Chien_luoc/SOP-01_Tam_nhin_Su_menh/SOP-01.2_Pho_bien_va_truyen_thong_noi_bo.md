# SOP-01.2: PHỔ BIẾN VÀ TRUYỀN THÔNG NỘI BỘ

## 1. MỤC ĐÍCH
Quy định quy trình truyền đạt và phổ biến tầm nhìn, sứ mệnh và giá trị cốt lõi đến toàn bộ stakeholders, đảm bảo:
- Mọi người hiểu rõ và ghi nhớ V/M/V
- Tạo sự hứng thú và cam kết
- Xây dựng momentum cho implementation
- Tạo alignment trong toàn tổ chức
- Embedded vào culture từ ngày đầu

## 2. PHẠM VI ÁP DỤNG
Áp dụng cho:
- Ban Giám hiệu (leaders of the launch)
- Phòng Marketing/Communications
- Phòng Nhân sự
- Toàn thể nhân viên
- Học sinh và phụ huynh
- Đối tác và cộng đồng

## 3. NGUYÊN TẮC TRUYỀN THÔNG

### 3.1. Multi-channel
Sử dụng nhiều kênh để reach mọi người:
- Face-to-face (most powerful)
- Print materials
- Digital channels
- Visual displays
- Interactive activities

### 3.2. Multi-touch
Lặp lại message nhiều lần:
- Rule of 7: Người ta cần nghe 7 lần mới nhớ
- Spaced repetition: Repeat qua thời gian
- Different formats: Mỗi lần khác nhau để keep interest

### 3.3. Multi-level
Cascade từ trên xuống:
- Board → BGH → Managers → Teachers → Students
- Mỗi level là ambassador cho level dưới

### 3.4. Storytelling
Không chỉ truyền đạt information, mà kể stories:
- Why stories (lý do behind V/M/V)
- How stories (quá trình xây dựng)
- Example stories (minh họa values trong action)

### 3.5. Two-way
Không chỉ broadcast, mà listen:
- Q&A sessions
- Feedback channels
- Discussion forums
- Address concerns

## 4. CHIẾN LƯỢC TRUYỀN THÔNG

### 4.1. Launch Strategy - Big Bang

**Mục tiêu**: Tạo sự kiện lớn, memorable, tạo momentum

**Thời điểm**: Ngay sau khi được Board approve

**Concept**: "Grand Unveiling" - Như launch một sản phẩm mới

### 4.2. Sustain Strategy - Long Tail

**Mục tiêu**: Duy trì awareness và engagement sau launch

**Thời gian**: 6-12 tháng đầu intensive, sau đó ongoing

**Approach**: Regular touchpoints, integration vào activities

## 5. QUY TRÌNH TRIỂN KHAI

### 5.1. Pre-Launch: Chuẩn bị (2-4 tuần)

#### 5.1.1. Develop communication materials
**A. Core Messaging Document**:
```
1. V/M/V statements (official text)
2. Elevator pitch (30 giây)
3. Extended explanation (3-5 phút)
4. FAQs (10-15 questions)
5. Key messages
6. Talking points cho leaders
7. Soundbites và quotes
```

**B. Visual Identity Package**:
- Logo integration (nếu có logo mới/update)
- Typography và color palette
- Key visual/graphic representation
- Icons cho mỗi value
- Background images/photos
- Branded templates

**C. Print Materials**:
- **Poster** (A3, A2):
  - V/M/V statements prominent
  - Inspiring visuals
  - Để treo tại: offices, classrooms, hallways, canteen
  - Số lượng: 100-200 posters

- **Standee** (180cm x 80cm):
  - Cho lobby, auditorium, main areas
  - Số lượng: 5-10 standees

- **Banners** (1m x 2m hoặc 2m x 4m):
  - Outdoor/indoor spaces
  - Số lượng: 3-5 banners

- **Pocket cards** (credit card size):
  - V/M/V trên một mặt
  - Values definitions mặt kia
  - Cho mỗi nhân viên carry trong wallet
  - Số lượng: Số nhân viên x 2

- **Booklet/Handbook** (A5, 8-12 trang):
  - Comprehensive explanation
  - Stories và examples
  - Behaviors và expectations
  - Beautiful design
  - Số lượng: Mỗi nhân viên 1 cuốn

**D. Digital Assets**:
- **Video** (2-4 phút):
  - Giới thiệu V/M/V
  - Behind-the-scenes của quá trình xây dựng
  - Testimonials từ stakeholders
  - Inspiring visuals và music
  - Professional production

- **Website**:
  - Dedicated page cho V/M/V
  - Interactive elements
  - Stories library
  - Download materials

- **Email templates**:
  - Announcement email
  - Follow-up emails
  - Newsletter section template

- **Social media assets**:
  - Profile covers
  - Post graphics (10-15 designs)
  - Instagram stories template
  - Short video clips (15-60 giây)

- **Presentation templates**:
  - PowerPoint/Google Slides
  - Branded với V/M/V
  - Cho staff sử dụng

**E. Merchandise/Swag** (Optional):
- T-shirts với values printed
- Notebooks, pens
- Tote bags
- Stickers
- Pins/badges

**Production timeline**:
```
Week 1: Design và content creation
Week 2: Review và revisions
Week 3: Production/printing
Week 4: Quality check và distribution prep
```

#### 5.1.2. Plan Launch Event
**Format options**:
- **Option A**: All-staff meeting + separate student/parent events
- **Option B**: Community-wide celebration (staff + students + parents)
- **Option C**: Phased approach (staff first, then students, then parents)

**Recommended**: Option A (control message, target audience)

**Staff Launch Event** (2-3 giờ):
```
Location: Auditorium hoặc large space
Date: Đầu năm học (August) hoặc đầu năm dương (January)
Time: Trong giờ làm việc
Attendance: Mandatory cho all staff

Agenda:
00:00-00:15: Welcome, build excitement
00:15-00:30: Context: Why V/M/V matters
00:30-00:50: THE BIG REVEAL
            - Video teaser
            - Principal unveils V/M/V
            - Explanation và storytelling
00:50-01:00: Break
01:00-01:30: Deep dive: Each value
01:30-02:00: Q&A và discussion
02:00-02:15: Commitment ceremony (symbolic)
02:15-02:30: Celebration & distribution of materials
```

**Production elements**:
- Stage setup (backdrop với V/M/V)
- AV: Video, music, lighting
- Props: Sealed envelope, ceremonial unveiling
- F&B: Light refreshments
- Photographer/videographer
- Emcee/host

#### 5.1.3. Prepare Leaders
**Leadership briefing** (1 tuần trước launch):
- **Participants**: BGH + Trưởng phòng ban + Team leads
- **Duration**: 2-3 giờ
- **Content**:
  - Deep dive vào V/M/V
  - Rationale và decision-making process
  - Their role as ambassadors
  - How to talk about it
  - Handling questions và concerns
  - Practice sessions

**Talking points packet**:
- Key messages
- Sample responses to common questions
- Personal stories they can share
- Do's and Don'ts

**Cascade plan**:
- Who will communicate to whom
- Timeline
- Materials provided

### 5.2. Launch Phase (Tuần 0-2)

#### 5.2.1. Launch Day (Day 0)
**Morning**:
```
07:00-08:00: Setup (banners, standees, decorations)
08:00-08:30: Leadership team final prep
08:30-09:00: Staff early arrival, coffee & excitement building
09:00-11:30: LAUNCH EVENT (as per agenda above)
11:30-12:00: Immediate cascading to students (homeroom)
```

**Homeroom Announcement** (30 phút):
- Giáo viên homeroom explain V/M/V
- Age-appropriate explanation
- Interactive Q&A
- Distribute pocket cards (cho lớp lớn)

**Afternoon**:
```
13:00-14:00: Photo ops, media interviews
14:00-17:00: Department meetings (leaders cascade)
17:00: Email to parents (announcement)
```

**Email to Parents**:
```
Subject: Introducing Our Vision, Mission & Core Values

Dear [Name],

Today marks an exciting milestone for [School Name]. We are 
thrilled to unveil our Vision, Mission, and Core Values that 
will guide our journey forward.

[Insert V/M/V]

These statements reflect who we are, what we stand for, and 
where we're heading. They were developed through extensive 
input from our community, including parents like you.

Over the coming weeks, you'll see these values come to life 
in everything we do. We look forward to partnering with you 
to achieve our vision together.

[Link to website for more info]
[Link to video]

Warm regards,
[Principal name]
```

#### 5.2.2. Week 1: Intensive Communication
**Daily touchpoints**:
- **Day 1** (Launch day): As above
- **Day 2**: Morning assembly focus on 1 value
- **Day 3**: Department meetings - discuss implications
- **Day 4**: Newsletter special edition
- **Day 5**: Social media campaign kickoff

**Activities**:
**Morning Assemblies** (15 phút mỗi ngày):
```
Monday: Overview of V/M/V
Tuesday: Deep dive - Value 1
Wednesday: Deep dive - Value 2
Thursday: Deep dive - Value 3
Friday: Stories from students/staff
```

**Department Meetings**:
- What does V/M/V mean for our department?
- How do we live these values in our work?
- Brainstorm action items

**All-hands Q&A** (Online):
- Live Q&A session with Principal
- Submit questions in advance
- Address concerns transparently

**Website & Digital**:
- Homepage takeover
- Blog posts
- Video on loop in digital displays
- Email signatures updated

**Social Media Blitz**:
```
Day 1: Announcement post (video)
Day 2: Behind-the-scenes of development process
Day 3: Spotlight on Vision
Day 4: Spotlight on Mission
Day 5: Spotlight on Value 1
Day 6-10: Continue with other values
Day 11-14: Student/staff testimonials
```

Post frequency: 2-3 posts/day during launch week

#### 5.2.3. Week 2: Deepening Understanding
**Training Sessions** (1 giờ, by department):
- What each value means specifically for your role
- Expected behaviors
- Examples và non-examples
- Practice applying to scenarios
- Q&A

**Workshop**: "Living Our Values" (2-3 giờ):
- Interactive activities
- Case studies
- Role plays
- Group discussions
- Personal commitments

**Student Engagement**:
- **Elementary**: Arts & crafts (draw your favorite value)
- **Middle School**: Essay/poster competition
- **High School**: Panel discussion, student ambassador program

**Parent Events**:
- Coffee morning: Q&A with Principal
- Evening session cho working parents
- Video on parent portal

**Community Partners**:
- Email announcement
- Materials shared
- Invitation to align

### 5.3. Post-Launch: Sustaining Phase (Tuần 3 - Tháng 12)

#### 5.3.1. Month 1: Reinforcement
**Weekly touchpoints**:
- Monday assembly: Value of the Week
- Wednesday newsletter: Feature value in action
- Friday recognition: Shout-out to value champions

**Physical environment**:
- Ensure all materials up and visible
- Refresh any damaged items
- Add more visual reminders

**Integration begins**:
- Link to OKRs
- Include in job descriptions
- Add to performance reviews

#### 5.3.2. Months 2-6: Integration
**Regular features**:
- Monthly "Value Champion" awards
- Quarterly "Living Our Values" stories
- Biweekly tips on living each value

**Events**:
- Month 3: Review và check-in survey
- Month 6: Re-launch event (refresh momentum)

**Continuous content**:
- Collect stories monthly
- Share on newsletter, intranet, social media
- Build story library

#### 5.3.3. Months 7-12: Embedded
**Ongoing activities**:
- V/M/V part of new hire orientation
- Regular recognition tied to values
- Annual refresh campaign

**Measurement**:
- Awareness surveys (Month 3, 6, 12)
- Track KPIs
- Gather feedback

**Adjustments**:
- Refine communication based on feedback
- Add new stories và examples
- Update materials nếu needed

## 9. MEASUREMENT & EVALUATION

### 9.1. Awareness (Nhận thức)
**Metrics**:
- % staff nhớ đúng Vision: Target ≥ 90%
- % staff nhớ đúng Mission: Target ≥ 85%
- % staff nhớ ≥ 3 Core Values: Target ≥ 80%

**Method**: Survey (multiple choice + open-ended)

**Frequency**: Month 1, 3, 6, 12

### 9.2. Understanding (Hiểu biết)
**Metrics**:
- % staff giải thích đúng ý nghĩa: Target ≥ 75%
- % staff cho examples: Target ≥ 70%

**Method**: Survey (open-ended), focus groups

**Frequency**: Month 3, 6, 12

### 9.3. Engagement (Tham gia)
**Metrics**:
- Event attendance rate
- Content engagement (views, shares, comments)
- Voluntary participation in activities

**Method**: Tracking, analytics

**Frequency**: Ongoing

### 9.4. Sentiment (Cảm xúc)
**Metrics**:
- Positive sentiment score: Target ≥ 80%
- Inspiring rating: Target ≥ 4.0/5.0

**Method**: Survey, social listening

**Frequency**: Month 3, 6, 12

### 9.5. Behavior (Hành vi)
**Metrics**:
- % staff self-report living values: Target ≥ 75%
- Peer recognition frequency
- Values-based decisions observed

**Method**: Survey, observations, anecdotes

**Frequency**: Month 6, 12

## 12. FAQ

**Q1: Launch trong năm hay đầu năm học?**
A: Ideal là đầu năm học (August/September). Nhưng nếu sẵn sàng sớm hơn, có thể launch mid-year (January). Avoid cuối năm học.

**Q2: Bắt buộc participation không?**
A: Launch event nên mandatory cho staff. Các activities khác có thể optional nhưng highly encouraged.

**Q3: Xử lý resistance như thế nào?**
A: 
- Listen to concerns
- Address transparently
- Private conversations với resisters
- Find early adopters làm champions
- Show benefits clearly

**Q4: Multi-language school thì sao?**
A: 
- V/M/V trong cả hai ngôn ngữ
- Materials bilingual
- Events có translation nếu cần
- Culturally sensitive messaging

**Q5: Nếu budget limited?**
A: Prioritize:
1. Leadership preparation (critical)
2. Launch event (create momentum)
3. Basic print materials (visibility)
4. Training (understanding)
5. Digital/video (nice to have)
