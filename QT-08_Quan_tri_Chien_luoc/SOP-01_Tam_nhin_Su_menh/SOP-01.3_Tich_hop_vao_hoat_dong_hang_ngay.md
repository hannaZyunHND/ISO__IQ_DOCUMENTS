# SOP-01.3: TÍCH HỢP VÀO HOẠT ĐỘNG HÀNG NGÀY

## 1. MỤC ĐÍCH
Quy định cách thức tích hợp tầm nhìn, sứ mệnh và giá trị cốt lõi vào các hoạt động, quy trình và quyết định hàng ngày của trường, đảm bảo:
- V/M/V không chỉ là lý thuyết mà là hành động
- Mọi người thấy V/M/V "sống" trong công việc
- Quyết định được đưa ra dựa trên values
- Văn hóa tổ chức được củng cố liên tục
- Alignment giữa nói và làm

## 2. PHẠM VI ÁP DỤNG
Áp dụng cho:
- Ban Giám hiệu
- Tất cả phòng ban và bộ phận
- Toàn thể cán bộ, giáo viên, nhân viên
- Học sinh (gián tiếp)

## 3. NGUYÊN TẮC TÍCH HỢP

### 3.1. Systemic Integration
Tích hợp vào hệ thống, không phải ad-hoc:
- Embedded trong quy trình chính thức
- Part of standard operating procedures
- Được measured và monitored

### 3.2. Visible and Tangible
Có thể nhìn thấy và cảm nhận được:
- Observable behaviors
- Concrete examples
- Visible consequences (rewards/recognition)

### 3.3. Consistent and Persistent
Nhất quán và kiên trì:
- Không on-again, off-again
- Applied consistently across all levels
- Sustained over time

### 3.4. Relevant and Practical
Liên quan và thực tế:
- Áp dụng được trong công việc hàng ngày
- Not abstract or theoretical
- Clear guidelines

## 4. CÁC ĐIỂM TÍCH HỢP CHÍNH

### 4.1. Strategic Planning & OKRs

#### 4.1.1. Liên kết Vision với Strategic Goals
**Quy trình**:
```
Vision: "To be the leading international school..."
    ↓
Strategic Goal 1: Academic Excellence
    ↓
Annual OKR: Increase average student achievement by 10%
    ↓
Departmental OKR: Implement new STEM curriculum
    ↓
Individual OKR: Teacher trains in STEM pedagogy
```

**Checklist khi lập OKR**:
- [ ] Có support vision không?
- [ ] Có align với mission không?
- [ ] Có reflect values không?
- [ ] Nếu đạt được, có đưa ta gần hơn đến vision không?

#### 4.1.2. Values-based Strategy Screen
**Khi đánh giá strategic initiatives**:
```
Proposed Initiative: Launch online learning program

Values Check:
✅ Excellence: Does it maintain quality standards?
✅ Innovation: Is it innovative approach?
✅ Care: Does it serve student needs?
✅ Collaboration: Does it foster teamwork?
✅ Integrity: Is it honest and transparent?

Decision: Proceed if aligned với majority of values
```

**Template**:
```
Initiative: [Name]
Strategic Fit: [High/Medium/Low]
Values Alignment:
- Value 1: [✅/❌] [Explanation]
- Value 2: [✅/❌] [Explanation]
- Value 3: [✅/❌] [Explanation]
Overall Alignment Score: [X/5]
Recommendation: [Go/No-go/Modify]
```

### 4.2. Hiring & Onboarding

#### 4.2.1. Job Descriptions
**Structure**:
```
[Position Title]

About Us:
[Insert 1 paragraph về school V/M/V]

Role Overview:
[Traditional JD content]

What We're Looking For:
In addition to qualifications, we seek candidates who:
- Demonstrate [Value 1] through [specific behaviors]
- Show commitment to [Value 2] by [examples]
- Embrace our mission to [mission statement]

How to Apply:
In your application, please share an example of how you've 
demonstrated one of our core values in your work.
```

**Example**:
```
About Us:
At ABC International School, we pursue excellence, embrace 
innovation, and care deeply about every student. Our mission 
is to empower students to reach their full potential and make 
positive contributions to society.

What We're Looking For:
- Demonstrate EXCELLENCE through consistently high-quality 
  teaching and continuous professional development
- Show INNOVATION by trying new pedagogies and technologies
- Embody CARE by building strong relationships with students
```

#### 4.2.2. Interview Process
**Add values-based questions**:

**Behavioral Questions by Value**:

**Excellence**:
- "Tell me about a time you went above and beyond expectations."
- "Describe how you ensure quality in your work."
- "What's your approach to continuous improvement?"

**Integrity**:
- "Share a situation where you had to make an ethical decision."
- "Have you ever witnessed misconduct? How did you handle it?"
- "How do you build trust with colleagues and students?"

**Innovation**:
- "Tell me about a time you introduced a new idea or approach."
- "How do you stay current with trends in education?"
- "Describe a creative solution you developed for a problem."

**Collaboration**:
- "Share an example of successful teamwork you led or participated in."
- "How do you handle disagreements with colleagues?"
- "What's your approach to cross-functional projects?"

**Care**:
- "Tell me about a struggling student you helped."
- "How do you build relationships with diverse learners?"
- "What does student-centered teaching mean to you?"

**Scoring Rubric**:
```
For each value:
1 = No evidence
2 = Weak evidence
3 = Moderate evidence
4 = Strong evidence
5 = Exceptional evidence

Total Values Score: [X/25]
Minimum threshold: 15/25 to proceed
```

#### 4.2.3. Onboarding Program
**Day 1 - Values Immersion** (2 giờ):
```
09:00-09:30: Welcome & School History
09:30-10:00: Deep Dive into V/M/V
            - What each means
            - Why they matter
            - How they were developed
10:00-10:30: Values in Action
            - Video examples
            - Stories from staff
10:30-11:00: Your Role in Living Values
            - Expectations
            - Resources
            - Q&A
```

**30-60-90 Day Check-ins**:
```
30 Days:

- Observed values in action? (examples)
- Questions or concerns?

60 Days:
- Applied values in your work? (examples)
- Alignment comfort level?
- Support needed?

90 Days:
- Full assessment of values fit
- Mutual decision on continuation
```

### 4.3. Performance Management

#### 4.3.1. Goal Setting (Annual)
**Format**:
```
Employee: [Name]
Role: [Position]

Part A: Performance Goals (60%)
[Traditional SMART goals linked to OKRs]

Part B: Values-Based Goals (40%)
For each core value, set 1 specific goal:

Value 1 - EXCELLENCE:
Goal: [Specific behavior or outcome]
How measured: [Metric or indicator]
Target: [Quantifiable target]

Value 2 - INNOVATION:
Goal: [e.g., Implement 1 new teaching method]
How measured: [Student feedback, observation]
Target: [Rating ≥ 4/5]

[Continue for each value]
```

**Example**:
```
Value: CARE
Goal: Build stronger relationships with all students
How measured: 
- Student survey on teacher relationship
- Parent feedback
- Observation notes
Target: 
- Survey score ≥ 4.5/5
- Zero parent complaints about responsiveness
- At least 3 documented examples of going extra mile
```

#### 4.3.2. Performance Reviews (Biannual)
**Review Structure**:
```
1. Performance Goals Review (60%)
   [Traditional review]

2. Values Assessment (40%)
   For each value:
   
   Value: [Name]
   Self-Assessment: [1-5 scale + examples]
   Manager Assessment: [1-5 scale + examples]
   Peer Feedback: [Anonymous, 2-3 peers]
   Evidence:
   - [Specific behaviors observed]
   - [Stories/examples]
   - [Impact on others]
   
   Strengths:
   [What's going well]
   
   Development Areas:
   [Where to improve]
   
   Action Plan:
   [Specific steps for next period]

3. Overall Values Rating: [Average of all values]
4. Overall Performance Rating: [60% performance + 40% values]
```

**Rating Scale**:
```
5 - Role Model: Exemplifies value, inspires others
4 - Exceeds: Consistently demonstrates, strong examples
3 - Meets: Regularly demonstrates, adequate
2 - Developing: Inconsistent, needs improvement
1 - Does Not Meet: Rarely demonstrates, concern
```

**Consequences**:
- Overall rating < 2.5 → Performance Improvement Plan
- Overall rating < 2.0 → Consider termination
- Values rating significantly below performance → Coaching

#### 4.3.3. 360-Degree Feedback
**Include values assessment**:
```
[Name] demonstrates [Value] by:
1 = Never  2 = Rarely  3 = Sometimes  4 = Often  5 = Always

Excellence:
- Consistently delivers high-quality work: [1-5]
- Strives to exceed expectations: [1-5]
- Seeks feedback and improves: [1-5]

[Continue for each value with 3-5 items]

Open-ended:
- Share an example of when [Name] exemplified our values.
- Any suggestions for improvement?
```

### 4.4. Recognition & Rewards

#### 4.4.1. Values-Based Recognition Program
**"Living Our Values" Awards**:

**Frequency**: Monthly

**Categories**: 
- One award per value per month
- Open nominations (anyone can nominate)

**Nomination Form**:
```
Nominee: [Name]
Nominated by: [Your name]
Value: [Select one]
Situation: [Describe what happened]
Action: [What did they do?]
Impact: [What was the result?]
Why this exemplifies [Value]: [Explanation]

Attachment: [Photo, email, evidence if applicable]
```

**Selection Process**:
- Review committee (HR + 2 rotating staff members)
- Review all nominations
- Select winners based on:
  - Clarity of value demonstration
  - Significance of impact
  - Consistency with value definition

**Recognition**:
- Certificate presentation (all-staff meeting or assembly)
- Gift card (500k-1 triệu VNĐ)
- Feature in newsletter with photo và story
- Name on "Values Champions" wall
- Small trophy/plaque

**Annual "Values Champion" Award**:
- Based on cumulative monthly awards
- Special recognition at end-of-year celebration
- Significant prize (10-20 triệu VNĐ equivalent)
- Permanent plaque

### 4.5. Decision Making

#### 4.5.1. Decision Framework
**Values-Based Decision Checklist**:
```
Decision: [What are we deciding?]
Options: [List 2-3 options]

For each option, assess:

1. Mission Alignment:
   How well does this serve our mission?
   Rating: [1-5]
   Explanation: [...]

2. Values Alignment:
   Value 1 [Name]: [✅/⚠️/❌] [Why?]
   Value 2 [Name]: [✅/⚠️/❌] [Why?]
   Value 3 [Name]: [✅/⚠️/❌] [Why?]
   [Continue for all values]
   
3. Vision Contribution:
   Does this move us toward our vision?
   Rating: [1-5]
   Explanation: [...]

4. Stakeholder Impact:
   Students: [Positive/Neutral/Negative]
   Staff: [Positive/Neutral/Negative]
   Parents: [Positive/Neutral/Negative]

5. Overall Score: [Calculate]

Recommendation: [Option with highest alignment]
Trade-offs: [Any values tensions?]
Mitigation: [How to address concerns?]
```

**Example Application**:
```
Decision: Accept donation of 5 tỷ VNĐ from company X 
          with condition to name building after CEO

Option A: Accept with naming rights
Option B: Accept without naming rights (negotiate)
Option C: Decline

Values Check:
- EXCELLENCE: ✅ Funds improve facilities
- INTEGRITY: ⚠️ Does CEO's reputation align with ours?
              Need due diligence
- CARE: ✅ Better facilities benefit students
- INNOVATION: ✅ Enables new programs

Decision: Option B if CEO reputation OK, else Option C
```

#### 4.5.2. Meeting Practices
**Start Meetings with Values Check-in** (5 phút):
```
Facilitator: "Before we begin, let's do a values check-in. 
Which value will be especially important in today's discussion?"

Team identifies 1-2 relevant values
Brief discussion on what that means for today
Keep visible during meeting (write on board)

At end of meeting:
"How well did we live [Value] today?"
Quick reflection
```

**Example**:
```
Meeting: Budget allocation discussion
Values focus: INTEGRITY (transparency), CARE (student impact)

During meeting:
- Reference values when making points
- "This aligns with our care for students because..."
- Check decisions against values

End reflection:
- "We demonstrated integrity by sharing all information openly"
- "We showed care by prioritizing student programs"
```

#### 4.5.3. Conflict Resolution
**Values-Based Mediation**:
```
When conflicts arise:

1. Identify the issue
2. Ask: "What values are in tension here?"
3. Each party explains their perspective through values lens
4. Find common ground in shared values
5. Explore solutions that honor multiple values
6. Agree on path forward aligned with values
```

**Example**:
```
Conflict: Teacher wants to try new grading system vs.
          Principal wants consistency

Values at play:
- INNOVATION (try new things) vs.
- EXCELLENCE (maintain standards)

Resolution: 
Pilot new system with one class (innovation)
Rigorous assessment of results (excellence)
Decision based on data (integrity)
```
