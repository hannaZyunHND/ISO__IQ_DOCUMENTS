# SOP-06.2: LẬP KẾ HOẠCH VÀ DỰ TOÁN ĐẦU TƯ

## 1. MỤC ĐÍCH
Quy định quy trình lập kế hoạch chi tiết và dự toán đầu tư cho cơ sở mới, đảm bảo:
- Business plan toàn diện và realistic
- Financial model chính xác
- Funding strategy khả thi
- Rủi ro được đánh giá và quản lý
- Approval process rõ ràng

## 2. QUY TRÌNH LẬP KẾ HOẠCH

### 2.1. Bước 1: Business plan development (4-6 tuần)

#### 2.1.1. Cấu trúc Business Plan
```
BUSINESS PLAN - CƠ SỞ MỚI (80-120 trang)

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (5-8 trang)

1. OPPORTUNITY (10 trang)
   - Market analysis summary
   - Competitive advantage
   - Target market

2. STRATEGY (10 trang)
   - Positioning
   - Program offerings
   - Differentiation
   - Go-to-market strategy

3. OPERATIONS PLAN (15 trang)
   - Facility specifications
   - Capacity plan
   - Staffing plan
   - Academic program
   - Support services

4. MARKETING PLAN (10 trang)
   - Brand strategy
   - Marketing mix
   - Enrollment strategy
   - Budget

5. MANAGEMENT TEAM (5 trang)
   - Organizational structure
   - Key hires
   - Governance

6. FINANCIAL PLAN (20 trang)
   - Investment requirements
   - Revenue projections
   - Operating expenses
   - Cash flow
   - Profitability

8. IMPLEMENTATION PLAN (10 trang)
   - Timeline

   - Critical path

```

### 2.4. Bước 4: Sensitivity analysis (1 tuần)

#### 2.4.1. Scenarios
```
Base case (Most likely):
- Enrollment: As projected
- Tuition: As planned
- Costs: As budgeted
- IRR: 16%

Optimistic case:
- Enrollment: +20%
- Tuition: +5%
- Costs: -5%
- IRR: 22%

Pessimistic case:
- Enrollment: -20%
- Tuition: -5%
- Costs: +10%
- IRR: 8%

Worst case:
- Enrollment: -40%
- Tuition: -10%
- Costs: +20%
- IRR: Negative
```

#### 2.4.2. Break-even analysis
```
Calculate:
- Break-even enrollment

- How much buffer?
- Comfortable level?
```

### 2.5. Bước 5: Approval (2-3 tuần)

#### 2.5.1. Presentation to Board
```
Deck (30-40 slides):
1. Opportunity (5 slides)
2. Strategy (5 slides)
3. Market validation (5 slides)
4. Financial projections (8 slides)
5. Risks và mitigation (5 slides)
6. Implementation plan (5 slides)
7. Funding request (3 slides)
8. Recommendation (2 slides)
9. Q&A
```

#### 2.5.2. Board decision
```
Options:
1. Approve as proposed
2. Approve with conditions
3. Request more information
4. Defer decision
5. Reject

If approved:
- Authorize funding
- Appoint project team
- Set governance
- Proceed to next phase
```
