#### A. FILE STRUCTURE

```
Campaign_[Name]/
├── Images/
│   ├── Facebook/
│   ├── Instagram/
│   └── Website/
├── Videos/
│   ├── Facebook/
│   ├── Instagram/
│   └── YouTube/
├── Copy/
│   ├── Facebook_Ads/
│   ├── Google_Ads/
│   ├── Email/
│   └── Website/
└── Print/
    ├── Posters/
    └── Banners/
```

#### B. NAMING CONVENTION

**Images:** [Campaign]_[Type]_[Version]_[Date].jpg
- Example: TS2025_Facebook_Ad_v1_20250115.jpg

**Videos:** [Campaign]_[Type]_[Version]_[Date].mp4
- Example: TS2025_Intro_Video_v1_20250115.mp4

**Copy:** [Campaign]_[Channel]_[Type]_[Version].docx
- Example: TS2025_Facebook_Ad_Copy_v1.docx
